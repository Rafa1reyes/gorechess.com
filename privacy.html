Privacy Policy

Last updated 30 July 2026 · Spindola Software · spindola.software@gmail.com

What we collect

Account. When you first open GoreChess an anonymous account is created for you, identified by a random ID. We do not ask for your real name, email address, or phone number to play.

Profile you choose. The handle and avatar you pick. Your handle is visible to other players you are matched against.

Game data. Your rating, wins, losses, draws, games played, and in-game capture count, plus records of matches you play.

Purchases. If you buy a Tactical Nuke we store which store it came from, the store's transaction identifier, the amount, and whether it was granted or refunded.

What we do not collect

  • We never see your card details. Payments are handled entirely by Google Play Billing or by Stripe. Your card number never reaches our servers.
  • No location data.
  • No contacts, photos, files, camera, or microphone access.
  • No advertising identifiers. GoreChess contains no ads.
  • No third-party analytics or tracking.

Who we share it with

We do not sell your data and we do not share it for advertising. We use these processors to run the game:

Google Play BillingPayments in the Android appHandled by Google under its own policy

Your handle, avatar, and rating are shown to other players when you are matched with them — that is the point of a multiplayer game.

How long we keep it

Account and game data are kept while your account exists. Purchase records are kept as long as required for tax and accounting purposes, typically seven years.

Deleting your account

Email spindola.software@gmail.com and ask. We will delete your account, profile, and game history. Purchase records are retained where the law requires it.

Children

GoreChess depicts stylised cartoon violence and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. If you believe a child has created an account, email us and we will remove it.

Changes

If this policy changes we will update the date above and post the new version here.
